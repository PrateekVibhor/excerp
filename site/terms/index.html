Skills lists

  • A skills list is made for a job role you choose, and from a job posting if you paste one. It is written with the help of an AI language model and web searches, and it can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Check anything that matters before you rely on it.
  • Each skill in a list is rated STRONG, THIN or MISSING. STRONG means we found two or more links, at least one of them from our library of sources. THIN means one link, or only links found on the web. MISSING means no link yet. Library links come from sources we checked when we added them; web links are found automatically and are not reviewed by us. A rating describes the links we found, not your own skills or what any employer wants.
  • A list can carry a note headed THIN EVIDENCE. It means the list was built from fewer than eight similar job postings, and sometimes only from the posting you pasted, so there is less to check it against. The note is about the whole list and is separate from each skill's rating.
  • Links lead to websites run by other people. We do not control them and cannot promise they are accurate, safe or still available.
  • A skills list is general information to help you plan what to learn. It is not career, hiring, legal or professional advice, and it does not promise a job, an interview or any result.
  • Paste only job postings you are allowed to use, and do not paste other people's personal information (names, contact details, résumés). You keep ownership of what you paste; you allow us to store it and send it to the outside services named in the Privacy Policy to build your list.
  • We may limit how many lists you can build each day.

You must be 18 or older to use these two features. When you turn either on, you confirm that you are, and you accept these terms. If you are not 18 or older, leave them off.
